Property Verification

Property verification is a detailed process where we examine ownership records, link chain documents, and Encumbrance Certificates to ensure the property is free from disputes, fraud, or hidden liabilities.

Link chain documents trace the history of ownership transfers. At InRealtor, we verify every link to confirm that the property has a clear and legal title, preventing future ownership conflicts.

An Encumbrance Certificate (EC) shows whether a property has any legal dues, loans, or mortgages. We obtain and validate ECs to guarantee that the property is free from financial or legal encumbrances.

Our verification covers ownership authenticity, title clarity, encumbrances, litigation history, property tax records, building approvals, RERA compliance, mutation entries, and more — a comprehensive 21‑point checklist designed to protect your investment.

By conducting thorough checks upfront, we prevent costly disputes, fraudulent transactions, and delays. Our expert team ensures you only proceed with properties that are legally sound and financially safe.
