Statutory Compliance Assistance

At InRealtor Hyderabad, we recognize that statutory compliance is one of the most critical aspects of property buying. Without proper approvals and adherence to government regulations, buyers risk facing legal disputes, penalties, or even cancellation of ownership rights. That’s why our statutory compliance assistance services are designed to provide complete peace of mind by ensuring every property transaction meets the required legal standards. We guide buyers through essential approvals such as building permits, occupancy certificates, completion certificates, and municipal clearances, while also verifying compliance with RERA regulations, zoning laws, and environmental norms. Our team liaises with local authorities to confirm that the property is free from acquisition notices, encumbrances, or violations of statutory rules. By conducting thorough checks on stamp duty payments, registration records, and mutation entries, we ensure that ownership is legally recognized and protected.

Unlike traditional brokers, InRealtor offers a buyer‑centric approach, where every compliance step is explained transparently, saving clients from hidden risks and future complications. Whether you are purchasing flats, villas, or plots in Hyderabad, our statutory compliance guidance guarantees that your investment is legally sound, secure, and future‑proof. At InRealtor, we believe that *dreams are realised when decisions are compliant*, and our expertise ensures your property journey is built on a foundation of trust and legality.

What Makes Statutory Compliance Assistance Different at InRealtor

At InRealtor, we believe to equip you with information that is important for a buyer to know, right question that needs to be asked from seller, documents that needs to be looked into. This will help

  • Building Permit Approval – Ensuring construction plans are sanctioned by municipal authorities.
  • Occupancy Certificate (OC) – Confirming the property is legally fit for occupation.
  • Completion Certificate (CC) – Validating that construction is completed as per approved plans.
  • RERA Compliance – Checking registration under the Real Estate Regulatory Authority for transparency.
  • Zoning & Land Use Regulations – Verifying property use aligns with local zoning laws.
  • Environmental Clearances – Ensuring compliance with environmental and pollution control norms.
  • Municipal Clearances – Confirming approvals from local municipal bodies for utilities and services.
  • Stamp Duty & Registration Compliance – Checking proper payment and legal recording of ownership.
  • Mutation Entries – Ensuring ownership records are updated in municipal and revenue records.
  • Property Tax Records – Confirming all dues are cleared and up to date.
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) – Validating the property is free from loans, mortgages, or legal dues.
  • Government Notifications – Ensuring property is not under acquisition or restricted development zones.
  • Utility Approvals – Verifying water, electricity, and sewage connections are legally sanctioned.
  • Boundary & Survey Records – Matching physical boundaries with official survey maps.
  • Society/Association Approvals – Checking permissions from housing societies or associations where applicable.
